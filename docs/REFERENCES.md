# References for the paper (from the author's literature review, 9 Nov 2026)

Status: entries and key figures come from the author's annotated literature review ("The Hidden Costs of Book Identification", 23 sources) and a second review note. I have not opened these sources myself unless marked [opened]. Check every figure against the original before submission. Current (2025–2026) rules and platform terms must not be presented as rules in force in 2015.

## Academic
1. Zhu, K., Shi, Q. & Banerjee, S. (2025). Monetizing platforms: an empirical analysis of supply and demand responses to entry costs in two-sided markets. *Management Science*. doi:10.1287/mnsc.2023.01390. (Goodreads Giveaways $119 fee from 9 Jan 2018; 101,684 giveaways, 82,552 books; participation −47% Big Five, −81% independents, −96% self-publishing services; Big Five share ~12% → 30%; HHI ~0.01 → 0.03; niche genres −26.2% relative, popular +11%; DiD across 2015–16 vs 2017–18 windows.)
2. Dilevko, J. & Dali, K. (2006). The self-publishing phenomenon and libraries. *Library & Information Science Research* 28, 208–234. doi:10.1016/j.lisr.2006.03.003. (14,061 self-published/author-services titles in WorldCat; 42.8% held by one library, 93.4% by fewer than ten.)
3. Schroff, S. (2023/2024). Missing the point: the disappointed hope of self-publishing authors. *International Journal of Cultural Policy* 30. doi:10.1080/10286632.2023.2288584. (Network analysis of UK publishing; self-published authors remain dependent on intermediaries; platform power.)
4. Bradley, J., Fulton, B., Helm, M. & Pittner, K. A. (2011). Non-traditional book publishing. *First Monday* 16(8). doi:10.5210/fm.v16i8.3353. (Bowker 2010: 316,480 traditional vs 2,776,260 non-traditional titles, the latter including public-domain reprints; ISBN-based counts miss output; 41% of sampled self-published titles hard to distinguish from similar titles.)
5. Brynjolfsson, E., Hu, Y. J. & Smith, M. D. (2003). Consumer surplus in the digital economy: estimating the value of increased product variety at online booksellers. *Management Science* 49(11), 1580–1596. doi:10.1287/mnsc.49.11.1580.20580. ($731m–$1.03bn consumer surplus from variety in 2000.)
6. Holley, R. P. (2014). Self-publishing: a new challenge for universal bibliographic control. IFLA WLIC 2014. digitalcommons.wayne.edu/slisfrp/124/.

## Industry / journalism
7. Society of Authors & Writers' Guild of Great Britain (2022). *Is It a Steal? An investigation into hybrid / paid-for publishing services*. writersguild.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/REPORT-Is-it-a-steal.pdf. (Voluntary survey of 240 writers, 2021; UK median payment £2,000; median sales 67; median royalties £68; median reported loss £1,861; 61 of 65 reporting a profit/loss outcome reported a loss; 59% no retail availability. Self-selected sample.)
